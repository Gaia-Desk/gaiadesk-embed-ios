# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- `GaiaDeskEmbed` for iOS 15+: share your app's own screen with your support team from a "Get help"
  button — consent enforced by the library, an always-visible "Sharing with … · Stop" indicator
  (frames only while it is on screen), masked views painted out before encoding (`.gaiaDeskMasked()`
  for SwiftUI), ReplayKit in-app capture or a window snapshot, view-only by default with an opt-in,
  partial guided mode through public UIKit API.
- Swift Package and CocoaPod; `HelpDeskUIKit` and `HelpDeskSwiftUI` examples.
